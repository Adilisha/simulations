Common test notes
  • Precipitate = an insoluble solid in the test tube.
  • Excess NaOH or NH3 can reveal soluble complex formation.
  • Flame observations are characteristic but can be affected by contamination.
  • Hg²⁺, Cd²⁺, and As³⁺ are highly toxic advanced examples. Do not handle these substances in a classroom practical.
